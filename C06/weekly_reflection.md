This week, I chose to use the same samples for all five sounds: percussion samples and a whistle that I recorded during weeks 3 and 4.

I first experimented extensively with the multislider to create original beats, always using the whistle as the final beat to mark the end of the loop. I then modified the function and amplitude modifier for sounds 4 and 5 so I could remove the whistle and experiment with higher or lower amplitudes without making these sounds too different from the other four, since they are all beats.
